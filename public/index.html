Project Viewpoint

Bridging the Generational Capital Gap

Traditional venture funds and banks ignore small ideas. We connect the two sides of the generational bridge.

1. The Youth Dilemma

Young creators, YouTube educators, and student engineers have viral potential and inventive prototypes, but they lack $500–$5,000 for critical gear or tooling. Traditional VC ignores checks this small, while bank credit requires collateral youth don't possess.

2. The Veteran Opportunity

Older generations, retired professionals, and senior industry executives possess decades of domain wisdom, industry contacts, and disposable capital. However, they lack transparent, modern avenues to discover, back, and mentor promising young creators directly.

3. The GenBridge Solution

An on-demand micro-investment network that operates like a food delivery app. Backers order $25 to $250 slices of gear milestones, attaching personalized mentorship advice. Funds are held in milestone escrow and disbursed as verified proof-of-work is submitted.

The Team Behind GenBridge

About Us

"We are team of 11 Students studying B.Tech in IIIT Bhopal."

We are undergraduates at the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Bhopal (IIIT Bhopal) — an Institute of National Importance under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. As young engineering students, we observed firsthand how brilliant college ideas, student hardware prototypes, and creative digital channels wither away simply because there is no accessible mechanism for small-ticket funding.

We designed and architected GenBridge to solve this fundamental imbalance. By fusing on-demand food delivery mechanics (menus, instant ordering, milestone courier tracking) with fintech micro-investing and cross-generational mentorship, we empower the next wave of builders across India and the globe.
